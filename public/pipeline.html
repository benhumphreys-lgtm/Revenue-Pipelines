How this is scored

What's live: Everything except the FY26 goal ($18M). Rep pipeline, weighted forecasts, non-rep owner totals, and current ARR all pull fresh from HubSpot on every reload.

What's manual: Only FY26_GOAL in the script config. Update it if the annual target changes; everything else auto-derives.

Coverage ratio = sum of open deal amounts ÷ rep target. Industry standard: <4x = Red, 4x–6x = Yellow, >6x = Green.

Weighted forecast = sum of (deal amount × HubSpot deal_close_probability) ÷ rep target. The probability is driven by the custom forecast_category property: Other = 25%, Possible = 50%, Likely = 75% (confirmed matching across all open rep deals). The Forecast Category row on each card shows the $ by category so you can verify the weighted number. <100% = Red, 100%–150% = Yellow, >150% = Green.

Weekly review signals: First meetings per week = leads that entered Top B stage during that week (HubSpot property hs_v2_date_entered_attempting_stage_id_745667965, same methodology as the Meeting Factory dashboard). A lead counts the week it was scheduled, even if it's currently in a later stage. Goal: 2 first meetings per rep per week (6/wk team). External email goal: 25 per rep per week. An email counts as external if ANY recipient (To/Cc/Bcc) is outside @prescriberpoint.com — this catches "Reply to teammate, Cc prospect" style outreach. Click any count (first meetings or emails) to open the live HubSpot list filtered to that rep.

Data hygiene note: If a rep's HubSpot inbox extension isn't connected, their outbound emails won't log to HubSpot and the activity count will understate reality. If a count looks suspiciously low, that's the first thing to check.

Stage breakdowns: Each rep card ends with two tables — Lead pipeline (open stages only: Top A → Mid B + Idle) and Deal pipeline (open Brand Direct deals by stage with count + $). Top A/Top B leads are highlighted since those are the active top-of-funnel progression targets. Every row is clickable and opens the HubSpot list filtered to that rep + that stage.

Combined confidence: both dimensions must be Yellow or better to qualify as "On Track." A rep with high coverage but low weighted = lots of stuck or low-probability deals; a rep with low coverage but high weighted = thin pipe with no margin for slippage.

Quarterly pacing: Open deals bucketed by closedate quarter. Pre-Q2 2026 closedates are flagged as stale (shown separately) and excluded from pacing counts.

Filter: pipeline 74379150 (Brand Direct), excluding stages Closed Won and Closed Lost. Stale-closedate flag = closedate before today.
